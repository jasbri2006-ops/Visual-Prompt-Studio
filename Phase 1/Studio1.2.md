**Project Tittle** Neon After Midnight
**Prompt** Create a digital illustration for a Synthwave and Retro 1980s album cover called “Neon After Midnight.” The main subject should be a futuristic sports car driving through a glowing city at night toward a bright neon horizon. Use a dark purple and blue sky with bright pink, orange, and blue neon lights. The car should be the largest object in the foreground so it becomes the main focal point.Use strong value and light contrast by placing very dark shadows around the edges of the composition and bright neon lights around the car and horizon. The brightest light should come from the glowing horizon and reflect onto the car, creating a dramatic nighttime mood. Add polished chrome surfaces to the car so the light and colors reflect across it.Create the illusion of space by making the car large and detailed in the foreground while buildings, lights, and geometric shapes become smaller as they move toward the horizon. Use overlapping buildings and objects to create depth. The neon grid on the ground should have larger lines in the foreground that become smaller and closer together in the distance.Show time and motion by adding glowing speed lines and light trails behind the car. The car should look like it is moving quickly toward the horizon. Use slight motion blur on the background lights while keeping the main car sharper so the movement is easy to see.Create unity by repeating the same neon purple, blue, pink, and orange colors throughout the album cover. Use geometric shapes, glowing lines, and reflections to connect the different parts of the composition. Add variety with one unexpected organic shape, such as a large glowing moon with a slightly irregular texture, to break up the repeated geometric patterns.Use an asymmetrical balance where the large car dominates the lower center of the composition while the bright glowing moon and buildings balance the visual weight on the opposite side. Make the car much larger than the background objects to create a clear difference in scale and hierarchy. Use sharp geometric lines, glowing light, deep shadows, reflections, and strong contrast to create an energetic 1980s futuristic atmosphere. Keep the overall composition clean enough that the viewer's eye is immediately drawn to the car and then follows the neon lines toward the horizon.
**Genre Choice** Synthwave/Retro 1980s
**Vocabulary Breakdown** Value is how light or dark something appears in an artwork it can help create contrast and make certain parts stand out. space is the area around, between, and inside objects in the artwork, artists can use space to make objects look closer or further away. Unity is when all the parts of art, work together and feel connected, repeating colors, shapes, or styles can help create unity. Balance is how the visual weight of an artwork is arranged.it can help make the artwork feel stable and no having one side feel too heavy. Rhythm is created when lines, shapes, colors, or other elements repeat throughout an artwork. It helps guide the viewer’s eyes around the artwork.
