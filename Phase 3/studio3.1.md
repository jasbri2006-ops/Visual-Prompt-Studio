**prompt 1** Historical Theme: Art and the afterlife Historical Period: Ancient Egypt Artwork Used as Inspiration: Funerary Mask of Tutankhamun; Canopic Jars; Fowling Scene from the Tomb of Nebamun Visual Prompt: Imagine a detailed scene inside an ancient Egyptian tomb. The focal point is the golden funerary mask of Tutankhamun, displayed in the center, surrounded by four canopic jars with the heads of the sons of Horus. The walls are decorated with vibrant paintings, including a fowling scene with the tomb owner and other figures hunting birds in a marsh. The artwork is rich in color, with gold, deep blues, reds, and earthy tones. The figures are shown in profile, following Egyptian art style, with flat planes and clear outlines. The composition is balanced, with the mask as the main emphasis and the jars and paintings framing it. This artwork was created to help the deceased in the afterlife by providing protection, food, and symbols of rebirth. It also showed the wealth and power of the person, and reflected the Egyptian belief in an eternal life after death.

The smooth, rounded shape and natural stone texture show the artist's focus on the female body and fertility. The sculpture is simple but powerful, using curves and volume to highlight the importance of reproduction and survival.
