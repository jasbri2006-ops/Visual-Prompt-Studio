**assignment tittle** Exploring Medium Through Prompt Variation
**prompt A** Create a watercolor painting of a mom and daughter getting ice cream on a warm evening. Show them outside an ice cream shop, with the daughter holding an ice cream cone and the mom smiling beside her. Include the shop, sidewalk, plants, and sunset. Emphasize line, shape, contrast, movement, texture, and emphasis. Use watercolor brushes, transparent washes, soft color changes, and wet-on-wet and wet-on-dry layering. Use darker colors to emphasize the mom and daughter. Add flowing lines and repeated plant shapes to create movement and unity. Keep some watercolor paper texture and soft, uneven edges.
**prompt B** Create a linocut print of the same mom and daughter getting ice cream on a warm evening. Show them outside an ice cream shop with the daughter holding an ice cream cone, along with the sidewalk, plants, and sunset. Emphasize line, shape, contrast, movement, texture, and emphasis. Use carving tools, bold lines, simple shapes, and strong light and dark areas. Make the mom and daughter stand out with darker shapes. Use flowing lines and repeated plant shapes to create movement and unity. Show the carved texture and slightly uneven edges of the linocut.
